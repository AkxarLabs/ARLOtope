# Propose

<!-- arlotope:contract (generated by `arlo docs` from workflow/) -->
**First, load the brief:** `uv run --with pyyaml python arlo.py brief propose --study <slug>`; skip when `ARLOTOPE BRIEF /propose` is already supplied. Follow its task ownership, additions and checkpoints.

**Finish by reporting one outcome** — `done`, `park`, `kill` — when the brief assigns this skill the task. Use `uv run --with pyyaml python arlo.py move <slug> <outcome> --note "…"`; follow the brief's routes. Never edit the journal or registry.

**It must produce:**
- studies/<slug>/proposal.md with the template's sections: headline hypothesis, frozen eval and analysis plan, staged plan with pre-written criteria and a final-evaluation row, budgets, kill criteria, §5 planned full-scale runs
<!-- /arlotope:contract -->

Output: `studies/<slug>/proposal.md` (from `templates/idea/proposal.md`).

## Procedure

1. Read `IDEA.md` and whichever of `lit-review.md` and `decisions.md` exist. With `decisions.md`, assemble
   §3 Method and §4 Experimental design from its settled decisions, and turn each OPEN decision into a
   pilot row whose criterion is "settles D-NNN". Without it, make the key design choices yourself and state
   each with a one-line rationale. Without a lit review, say in §2 that positioning was not checked.
2. Fill the template. These sections are **required**, because later procedures and checks read them:
   - **Headline hypothesis**: one sentence, the single central claim the novelty rests on, consistent with
     the `Headline: yes` decisions. `/spawn-project` copies it into PLAN.md and a LOOP_BRIEF, and it is
     the autonomy boundary: an `explore`-mode loop may reopen supporting decisions and expand the frontier
     under it, but abandoning it escalates to the PI.
   - **Frozen eval protocol**: primary metric, validation (selection) vs held-out test (reporting), defined
     now. The experiment loop never reads test. Design the split so that never reading test is physically
     easy to honour; its concrete form follows the project type chosen at spawn (the literal split for
     `ml`, pre-registration plus a hold-out or placebo for `empirical`, calibration moments for
     `simulation` — see `templates/project-types/`).
   - **The baseline** the comparison is against, with its tuning budget (budget-matched, or why not): the
     strongest fair baseline from the positioning, not the convenient one.
   - **Analysis plan** (frozen with the eval): the primary comparison, how uncertainty is reported and with
     how many replicates, exclusion rules, the decision rule (which result patterns mean supports / refutes
     / inconclusive, and the pre-agreed action for each) and the sanity controls (shuffled labels, a
     leakage check, a trivial baseline) with their expected ranges. Decide all of it before any number
     exists, so the result cannot choose its own test; pick the uncertainty method the field would accept
     and let the pilot's variance set the replicates. `/analyze` labels anything outside it exploratory
     (project rule analysis-plan); `checks/audit_prereg.py` reads it.
   - **Staged experiment table**: exp-001 is always a SMOKE, pilots answer the hypothesis cheaply, FULL
     runs are few. Every row gets its promotion/success criterion written now — criteria invented after
     seeing results are not criteria. It ends in one **final evaluation** row: the selected configuration
     on the held-out test split, read once (project rule test-once).
   - **Ablation plan**: every method component gets a removal test. With one component, plan the sanity
     ablations (e.g. against a random or shuffled control).
   - **Budgets** (compute + calendar), **§5 planned full-scale runs**, and **kill criteria**, concrete
     enough that a future session can apply them mechanically.
3. **Self-review once** against the lit review: would the authors of the closest work consider the
   comparison fair? Is the cheapest decisive experiment actually in the pilot stage?
4. **Report the outcome** (`arlo.py move <slug> <outcome> --note "…"`):
   - `done` — the proposal is complete. Note: the headline hypothesis and the total budget.
   - `kill` — writing it showed the idea can't be tested within the lab's means or isn't worth the cost.
     Record the reason in IDEA.md and harvest salvageable threads into `lab/knowledge/OPEN-QUESTIONS.md`.
   - `park` — sound, but not now (budget, data or hardware not available yet). Note: what it waits for.

   If `arlo move` says the study waits for the PI, present the proposal summary (hypothesis, plan table,
   budget, kill criteria, the planned full-scale runs of §5) and stop (headless:
   `needs_pi=<that checkpoint's id>`). The configured checkpoint decision maker supplies approval; the working agent cannot.
