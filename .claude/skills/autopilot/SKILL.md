# Autopilot Campaign

<!-- arlotope:contract (generated by `arlo docs` from workflow/) -->
**First, load the brief:** `uv run --with pyyaml python arlo.py brief autopilot [--study <slug>]`; skip when `ARLOTOPE BRIEF /autopilot` is already supplied. Follow its task ownership, additions and checkpoints.

**Show the PI** (`python tools/artifact.py publish --title "…" --file <path>`): the exit report, or a milestone that changes a decision. Publish once per milestone. Use `--question "…" --choices "A;B"` for feedback, never approval.

**Read first:** `AGENT-GUIDE.md` — "Programmatic agents", "Headless runs under the executor".
<!-- /arlotope:contract -->

A campaign follows the selected workflows within its approved scope and budget. It stops or waits
when those workflows require a decision; it never sends work outside the Lab.

## Choose the entry path

- **Dashboard campaign:** the form records authorization and the executor's keeper schedules cycles.
  Follow **Resume** and **Under the keeper** below. Do not interview the absent PI again.
- **New manual campaign:** read [Manual campaign setup](references/manual.md) to establish directions,
  budgets, concurrency and delegated checkpoints. No approved brief means no unattended campaign.
- **`continue <campaign-file>`:** resume that brief; do not create another campaign or change its bounds.

## Common rules

The ordinary research rules still apply: evidence, staged scale, frozen evaluations, budgets,
independent review and append-only ledgers. Every study follows its own workflow; paper production
is one default, not a requirement for all campaigns.

- Only the keeper may record a delegated approval, after checking current evidence, bounds and
  required checks. Other checkpoints wait for their configured decision maker. The working agent
  cannot approve its own work or widen the brief. Do not finalize or publish externally on your own.
- On a study's kill or results-stage exit, run `tools/guard.py evolve <slug>` and complete the
  applicable CORRECTION/RECIPE write-back before proceeding. Review items needing experiments
  run only within remaining budget; otherwise record them for the PI.
- If the brief authorizes explore mode, follow `/improve`'s expand/revisit boundary. Supporting
  decisions can reopen within bounds. Headline changes route through `/ideate --in-project`
  (or a successor hub idea when disabled), then `/propose`. `campaign_auto` requires the approved
  bounds and an overseer support check. It does not change frozen settings or itself approve a gate.
- Check directives and prior PI answers each portfolio pass. Record procedure completions in the
  Campaign Log and emit `cycle`; emit `approach_ideate` and `replan` when they occur, not just at exit.

## Resume

1. Read the named brief and check `arlo.py approved lab campaign:<name>`. If unapproved, stop.
2. Reconstruct state from the Campaign Log, `arlo.py status`, project registries and experiment logs.
   Treat the last log row as potentially incomplete; inspect its artifacts before repeating work.
3. Run project `scripts/status.py`: monitor a live run rather than duplicating it; record a dead one.
   Reconcile executor runs with `tools/executor_cli.py reconcile` when that path is enabled.
4. If a stop condition holds—deadline, budget, environment failure, or all studies waiting,
   shelved or finished—write the exit report. Otherwise take eligible work from each study's workflow.

## Under the keeper

The preamble identifies `CAMPAIGN CYCLE n of <campaign>`. One cycle is one portfolio pass.

1. Reconcile first. Add a new study's Campaign Log membership row before its first dispatch.
2. Dispatch the current procedure as its own run:
   `python tools/lab_bus.py emit campaign_dispatch --run-id $ARLOTOPE_RUN_ID --data skill=<procedure> --data target=<slug or hub>`
   Add `--data args="<args>"` when needed. Do not execute long project work in the coordinator,
   dispatch a waiting study, duplicate a run or start another campaign. The keeper checks membership,
   current step, launchability and parallelism, and handles retries. Do short bookkeeping here.
3. For a checkpoint delegated by the brief, have the overseer check the evidence against its bounds.
   Request the keeper's decision with
   `python tools/lab_bus.py emit campaign_approve --run-id $ARLOTOPE_RUN_ID --data study=<slug> --data checkpoint=<id> --data why="<reason>"`.
   The keeper rechecks authorization, membership, holds, escalations and checks. If approval advances
   the study, the keeper starts its next task; do not dispatch it again.
4. Ask only questions that cannot be resolved within the brief, with concrete options and a
   recommendation first. While the PI is away, the configured timeout policy may assume that
   recommendation for an ordinary question. Permissions and decisions outside the brief are never
   assumed. Record `needs_pi` with the study ID and continue other eligible studies.
5. End with the normal run footer and `--data campaign=continue|idle|done`. Use `done` when a stop
   condition holds or no target study can proceed. A cycle marked FINAL dispatches nothing.

The keeper is the scheduler. Do not depend on a harness-specific `/loop`, background the coordinator,
or enqueue project runs outside the keeper path.

## Exit report

Put a concise report at the top of the notebook entry:

1. Studies waiting, their decision/checkpoint and evidence. For a paper, include its headline run
   pointers, ensemble median Overall and unresolved review items.
2. Killed or parked ideas and reasons.
3. Decisions outside the brief that need the PI.
4. Recorded spend versus budget and the next command.

Complete the notebook and durable knowledge write-back even after an empty or interrupted campaign.
