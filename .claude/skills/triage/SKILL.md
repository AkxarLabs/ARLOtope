# Triage one idea

<!-- arlotope:contract (generated by `arlo docs` from workflow/) -->
**First, load the brief:** `arlo.py brief triage --study <slug>`; skip when `ARLOTOPE BRIEF /triage` is already supplied. It states how to finish, what to produce and show the PI, and the PI's additions and checkpoints.
<!-- /arlotope:contract -->

An idea that reaches this step came from you, from `/adopt`, or from a discussion — not from `/ideate`,
whose ideas are reflected and triaged as they are filed. Triage is `/ideate`'s **reflect** and **triage**
phases for this one idea, nothing more.

For an explicit replication, verification or exploratory question, judge value against that purpose.
An already-published result is expected in a replication; lack of novelty alone is not a reason to kill
it. Keep novelty scores honest and identify what uncertainty the requested work would resolve.

1. Read `studies/<slug>/IDEA.md` and the lab's knowledge it links (`lab/knowledge/`).
2. **Reflect.** Spawn `ideation.critics_per_idea` (`lab/config.yaml`) **`ideation-critic`** subagents in
   parallel, each in fresh context with only the idea's hypothesis and sketch and one of its charges
   (novelty, feasibility, then value, which also covers impact; defined in the role). Start each spawn's description with its charge
   ("Novelty skeptic: <idea>") so the PI can tell them apart. Run at most `ideation.reflection_rounds`
   rounds, revising the sketch between rounds only where a critique was answered by evidence.
3. **Triage.** Score novelty, feasibility, impact and cost-to-signal (1–10, anchored as in the IDEA.md template, each justified in one line) in
   IDEA.md's frontmatter, and write a "Reflection summary" in its triage notes: each critic's strongest
   surviving critique, so the literature review and scoping start with eyes open.
4. **Report the outcome** (`arlo.py move`, as the brief says):
   - `done` — the idea survives. Note: its strongest surviving critique.
   - `kill` — a critique it cannot survive (it is already done, it cannot be tested within the lab's means,
     it is not worth the cost). Harvest any surviving thread into `lab/knowledge/OPEN-QUESTIONS.md` first.
     Note: the reason.
   - `park` — not now, but maybe later. Note: what would bring it back.
